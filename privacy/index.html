Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Budgy is built with privacy at its core. We do not sell your data, use ads, or track you across apps and websites. Your financial information stays on your device and, if you use iCloud Sync, in your private iCloud account.

1. Information We Collect

Budgy does not collect or transmit your budgeting data, transactions, categories, savings goals, receipts, or other financial information to Budgy servers.

If you contact us by email or through our feedback form, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, feedback type, and message. Feedback sent from the app may also include basic support details such as the app version, build number, device model, and system version so we can diagnose issues and respond to your request.

2. Data Storage

All financial data you enter into Budgy is stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework. If iCloud Sync is enabled, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account via CloudKit.

Budgy may also store app preferences on your device, such as onboarding state, theme, currency, display settings, iCloud sync status, and purchase entitlement state. If you export a CSV or backup file, that exported file is created only when you choose to export it and is controlled by you.

3. iCloud

If you enable iCloud Sync, your data is subject to Apple’s privacy policies. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

4. Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple, Budgy may receive the Apple user identifier and, if Apple provides it, your name or email address. The Apple user identifier is stored securely on your device using Keychain so Budgy can restore your sign-in state. Budgy does not use Sign in with Apple to create a Budgy server account.

5. Receipt & Photo Scanning

Budgy can use your camera or selected photos to scan receipts or account screenshots and help fill in transaction details. Receipt images and recognized text are processed on your device using Apple frameworks and are not uploaded to Budgy servers.

6. Analytics & Tracking

Budgy does not include analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, tracking pixels, or third-party monitoring tools.

7. In-App Purchases

All purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information. Budgy checks App Store transaction status to unlock paid features and restore purchases.

8. How We Use Feedback Information

We use feedback information only to respond to your message, provide support, investigate bugs, improve Budgy, and protect the feedback form from spam or abuse. We do not sell feedback information or use it for advertising.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact

If you have any questions, contact us at hello@getbudgy.app.
